This Privacy Policy explains how Font Craft ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you use our free online font changer and text styling tool.

1. Data You Enter Into the Tool

All text you type into the font changer, title framer, or description editor is processed entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. We do not send this text to any server, and we do not log, store, or view what you type.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our site itself does not set tracking cookies. However, if we display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense or Monetag, those networks may place cookies or use similar technologies on your device to serve and measure ads. These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising Partners

If ads are shown on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor behavior, such as page views and general traffic sources. This data is anonymized and not linked to any text you type into the tool.

5. Children's Privacy

Our tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out through the Contact Us link on our homepage.
