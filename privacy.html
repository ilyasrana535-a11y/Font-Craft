This Privacy Policy explains how Font Craft ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you use our free online font changer and text styling tool.

1. Data You Enter Into the Tool

All text you type into the font changer, title framer, or description editor is processed entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. We do not send this text to any server, and we do not log, store, or view what you type.

2. Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, the services that deliver this site may receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not have accounts or sign-ups on this site.

  • Hosting: Font Craft is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs that include visitor IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our site itself does not set tracking cookies. However, if we display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense or Monetag, those networks may place cookies or use similar technologies on your device to serve and measure ads. These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising Partners

If ads are shown on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services on the Google partner sites page, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor behavior, such as page views and general traffic sources. This data is anonymized and not linked to any text you type into the tool. If we add analytics to the site, we will update this page.

6. Third-Party Links and Share Buttons

This site links to other websites, including our other projects (The Clarity Planner and Tech Finds Blog) and sharing options for WhatsApp, X (Twitter) and Facebook. The share buttons are plain links: they do not load any social network scripts on this site, and they only take you to that service when you click. Once you leave, the other site's own privacy policy applies, and we are not responsible for how other sites handle your information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or California), you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to ask what is held about you or to request deletion. Because we do not collect the text you type and do not run user accounts, we hold very little personal data ourselves. If you have a request, contact us at waqastechfinds@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at waqastechfinds@gmail.com.
