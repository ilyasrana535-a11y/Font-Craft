Can You Change Discord's Font?

Not officially. Discord uses its own font, called gg sans, across the whole app, and there is no font picker in the settings. People searching for a "Discord font changer" usually want one of two things, and they are different:

  • Change how the app looks. You cannot swap the app font. You can make chat text bigger or smaller with Chat Font Scaling in the Accessibility settings, and you can show text in a fixed-width font by putting it in a code block.
  • Change how your name or message looks. This is what the tool above does. It swaps normal letters for look-alike Unicode symbols, so the text keeps its style wherever you paste it.

You may see guides that use mods such as BetterDiscord to replace the app font. Those change the Discord client itself, and Discord's rules do not allow modifying the client, so using them puts your account at risk. Styled Unicode text needs no mods and nothing to install.

Username vs Display Name

Discord splits your identity into two names. The username is your unique handle: 2 to 32 characters, using lowercase Latin letters, numbers, underscores and periods, and never two periods in a row. The display name is what people see next to your messages, and it can be almost anything within Discord's Community Guidelines, including spaces, symbols and emoji, up to 32 characters.

That is why the fancy style belongs in the display name. When friends search to mention you, Discord matches your display name or your username, so a plain, simple username keeps you easy to find even with a decorative display name.

Tips Before You Change Your Name

  • Keep it readable. If friends cannot read your name in the member list, they will not recognize you.
  • Do not copy staff or other people. Discord's rules do not allow impersonating staff, groups or individuals, and a lookalike name can get you reported.
  • Preview on your phone. A style can look fine on a computer and show boxes on an older phone.
  • Mind accessibility. Screen readers can read styled letters as odd symbols, so keep important information in plain text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change Discord's font?

Not officially. Discord uses its own font and has no font setting. You can scale chat text under Accessibility settings, and you can paste styled Unicode text into names and messages.

Can I use a fancy font in my Discord username?

No. The unique username only allows lowercase Latin letters, numbers, underscores and periods. Put the fancy style in your display name or server nickname.

How long can a Discord display name be?

A display name can be 1 to 32 characters long and can include special characters and emoji.

Why does my styled name show boxes?

The device or app has no symbol for that character. It is rare on modern phones, but if it happens, try Bold or Sans Bold.

Will other people see my styled name on mobile?

Yes. Styled text is ordinary Unicode text, so it shows on phones and computers that have the symbols. Very old devices may show boxes for a few styles.
